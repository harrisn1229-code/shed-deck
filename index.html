Shed Deck

  1. Pick your cards. Turn decks on and open one to choose keys, qualities and voicings.
  2. Play what comes up. Tap the card to flip it and check the notes, shapes and sound.
  3. Rate it Nailed, Solid or Rusty. Ratings collect on the home screen so you can aim practice at what still needs work.

Auto-next

Deal the next card on a timer. The bar along the top of the card counts down, and pauses while the notes are showing.
